Spoken Output Analysis

What this shows

Your main issue is not vocabulary alone. You can express the commercial intention, but under speaking pressure your sentence structure switches back into English. This creates mixed-language output, unstable word order, and a tone that sounds less professional than your actual meaning.

Grammar & syntax Chinese structure collapses when the sentence becomes conditional.

Evidence: “If quantity more” appears inside the Chinese response.

What it means: The conditional frame 如果…就… is not automatic yet.

Word order The commercial condition is understandable but not naturally sequenced.

Evidence: “we can see price again” maps directly from English.

What it means: You need sentence frames before more vocabulary.

Vocabulary choice You insert English when Chinese retrieval slows down.

Evidence: “talk” and “discount” replace 商量 and 降价.

What it means: The words may be known passively, but not ready for speech.

Pragmatic tone The message needs a softer business tone.

Evidence: “不能太 discount” is clear, but too blunt for supplier negotiation.

What it means: You need polite pressure structures, not literal translation.

Chunking You are not retrieving reusable chunks as whole units yet.

Evidence: The response is built word by word.

What it means: Train chunks like 可以再商量 / 如果数量增加 / 价格还有空间吗.

Fluency / retrieval The pauses are probably caused by translation, not lack of intelligence.

Evidence: English connectors appear at the exact points where Chinese structure is needed.

What it means: Timed retrieval drills will help more than memorizing lists.

Specific issue 1 English lexical insertion

You said: “价格可以 talk 一下”

More natural: “价格可以再商量。”

This is a retrieval problem: the Chinese chunk was not ready fast enough.

Specific issue 2 Business tone too blunt

You said: “不能太 discount”

More natural: “但降价空间不能太大。”

The second version keeps the boundary while sounding more professional.

Suggested polished version

A stronger business response

价格可以再商量，但降价空间不能太大。如果数量增加，我们可以再一起看一下价格。

Training focus: structure-first business response drills + timed retrieval practice for conditional sentences.
